Guide

Everything the crew needs, in the order you'll need it.

1

Before you roll

  • Open hdmaps26.github.io on the phone that rides shotgun.
  • Add it to your home screen: Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or ⋮ → Add to Home screen on Android.
  • Split the signs into 2 bundles of 50, one for each street of your run.
  • Meet at HQ near Foster. Start between 6 and 10 AM.
2

Pick your run

  • Run 1: Lake Shore Drive south from Hollywood to North Ave, then the Red Line north to Bryn Mawr.
  • Run 2: Irving Park west from the lake, then Addison east back to the lake.
  • Both runs end near where they start, so nobody needs a ride back.
3

Drive to the next stop

  • The dark bar at the bottom always shows the next stop. Tap Directions to open Maps.
  • The map at the top marks the next stop with a ring. Green dots are done.
  • Each card's Look for line names a landmark at that corner.
4

Put up the 5 signs

  • Each stop lists 5 spots. Tap a spot when its sign is up.
  • Face signs toward drivers turning onto your street, or people walking out of a station.
  • The driver hops out, and the passenger takes the photo from the car.
  • When all 5 are done, the card turns green and the page jumps to the next stop.
5

Run 2 turnaround

California is the full loop. Western is about 25 minutes shorter. The 10 signs you'd have put at Rockwell and California show up as dashed extra spots at the busiest stops. Each street still gets 50.

6

Send photos

After each leg, upload the photos on the Photos page.

7

If something goes wrong

  • Spot blocked: use the same spot one block over, and keep it at the same stop.
  • Missed a stop: keep going and add its signs to the next stop.
  • Tapped by mistake: tap the spot again to undo it.
  • Two phones: ticks don't sync between phones, so one person does the tapping.
Stay legal: no signs on CTA property, on the Lake Shore Drive ramps, or inside parks. City code bars signs on poles and parkways. If a store owner says yes, a window or fence is the best spot.
