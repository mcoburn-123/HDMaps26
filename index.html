Guide

Everything the crew needs, in the order you'll need it.

1

Before you roll

  • Open this site on the phone that rides shotgun.
  • Add it to your home screen: Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or ⋮ → Add to Home screen on Android.
  • Load 50 signs. Meet at HQ near Foster. Start between 6 and 10 AM.
2

Drive to the next stop

  • Open Lake Shore. The dark bar at the bottom always shows the next stop. Tap Directions to open Maps.
  • The map marks the next stop with a ring. Green dots are done.
  • Each card's Look for line names a landmark at that corner.
3

Put up the 5 signs

  • Each stop lists 5 spots. Tap a spot when its sign is up. Tap again to undo.
  • Tap Photo next to the spot to snap the sign. A photo also ticks the spot.
  • Get the sign and a street sign or storefront in the same shot.
4

Sign off

  • Type your name in Placed by at each stop. After the first stop, it fills in for you.
  • The card turns green and the page jumps to the next stop. If a finished stop has no name, its box turns orange.
5

Send the report

  • When you're done, open Report and tap Send report.
  • It's one file with every stop, the names, the times and the photos. Text or email it to Eric.
6

If something goes wrong

  • Spot blocked: use the same spot one block over and tick it at the same stop.
  • Missed a stop: keep going and add its signs to the next stop.
  • Closed the page: everything stays saved on this phone. Just open it again.
  • Two phones: ticks don't sync between phones, so one phone keeps the record.
Stay legal: no signs on the Lake Shore Drive ramps, on CTA property, or inside parks. City code bars signs on poles and parkways. If a store owner says yes, a window or fence is the best spot.
